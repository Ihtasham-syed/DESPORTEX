PRIVACY POLICY

How DESPORTEX handles information you choose to send through this website.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you use our contact, quote or order inquiry forms, we may receive the information you provide, such as your name, email address or WhatsApp/phone number, country, product interests, estimated quantity, requirements, delivery information and any reference link you choose to include.

We do not ask for passwords, payment-card details, national identity numbers or other highly sensitive account credentials through these website forms. Please do not send that type of information in the message fields.

2. Why we use it

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, discuss custom manufacturing requirements, prepare quotations, follow up on requested orders, prevent abuse of our forms and maintain ordinary business records.

3. Form processing

Our website forms are processed through Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery service, so information you submit is transmitted to Web3Forms and then delivered to DESPORTEX. Web3Forms publishes its own privacy and data-processing information. You should only submit information you are comfortable sending through this service.

4. External services and links

The website may load product or editorial images from Pexels and provides links to services such as WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. When you load third-party content or follow an external link, that provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details according to its own privacy practices.

5. Cookies and analytics

The current DESPORTEX website does not set advertising cookies and does not include a third-party analytics or tracking platform. If analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking technologies are added later, this policy should be updated before they are enabled and any legally required consent controls should be implemented.

6. How long we keep information

DESPORTEX keeps business inquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the business relationship, maintain necessary records, resolve disputes or meet applicable legal obligations. Email providers and third-party processors may apply their own retention practices.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information submitted through the website. Information may be shared with service providers where needed to operate the inquiry process, or where disclosure is required by applicable law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures for this static website, including HTTPS at deployment, restrictive browser security policies and limited form fields. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your choices

You may contact DESPORTEX to ask about personal information you previously submitted, request correction, or request deletion where applicable. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact desporteximpex@gmail.com or WhatsApp +92 308 4223889.

This policy is provided as a practical website privacy notice and is not legal advice. Requirements can differ by the countries in which you operate or target customers.
