FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers about custom apparel, bulk orders and private-label development.

Do you accept bulk orders?

Yes. DESPORTEX is focused on B2B apparel orders and bulk buyers. Order requirements can be discussed based on the product and project.

Can I customize the products?

Yes. Customization can be discussed around the product, design, colors, branding and other agreed requirements.

Can I add my own logo?

Yes. Share your logo and branding requirements when requesting a quote so the details can be discussed.

Do you offer private labeling?

Yes. Private-label development is available for businesses looking to build apparel around their own brand identity.

Can I send my own design?

Yes. You can share designs, reference images or product requirements when starting a project.

How do I request a quote?

Use the Request a Quote page or contact DESPORTEX directly through WhatsApp or email.

How do I place an order?

Start by sharing your product and order requirements. DESPORTEX can then discuss the details and next steps with you.

Which countries do you work with?

DESPORTEX is positioned for B2B buyers, with a focus on customers trading in markets such as the USA and Europe.

Do you provide samples?

Sample requirements can be discussed depending on the product and project. Mention sampling when requesting your quote.
